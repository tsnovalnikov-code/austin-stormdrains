About this explorer

This page is a self-contained snapshot of the City of Austin FloodPro private drainage pipe layer (layer 8). The spreadsheet data, geometry and attributes are embedded directly in this HTML file.

How to use it

  • Filter by year built, width, height, length, subtype, material, shape, maintenance, watershed, record dates or free text.
  • Sort by any column, or use the sort control above the table.
  • Click a line on the map or a row in the table to inspect a segment.
  • “Fit filtered” zooms the map to the current result set; “Export CSV” downloads exactly the filtered rows.

Notes

  • Year built is the City's date-constructed field. About 10,350 segments have no year; width/height 0 values are treated as unknown.
  • Length is the GIS line length (SHAPE_LEN_ft), not the often-blank City length attribute.
  • All records are Active and Other (private) ownership. Many attributes are blank or “No Data”.
  • Data pulled —. Coordinates are WGS84 / EPSG:4326. Basemaps © OpenStreetMap contributors and Esri.
